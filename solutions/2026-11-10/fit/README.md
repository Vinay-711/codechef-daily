## Approach

The problem asks to calculate the total distance traveled in a week, which consists of 5 days. Each day, Chef travels to the office (X km) and back (X km), totaling 2 * X km per day. For 5 days, the total distance is 2 * X * 5 = 10 * X. The time complexity is O(T) and space complexity is O(1).
